## 4. UX findings

| Sev | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P3 | A new caption landed at the canvas centre, on top of a template's existing caption (Caption Stack). | **Fixed**: `CaptionPlacement` steps to the nearest free band; the video editor only counts captions on screen at the playhead. Both editors. |
| P3 | The Text sheet's "Your text" was real content, so typing added to it ("QA captionYour text"). | **Fixed**: for a new caption the default is the field's placeholder, the first keystroke replaces it, and the field takes focus (`CaptionTextDraft`). |
| P3 | A caption or sticker could be dragged entirely off the canvas, where only undo recovered it. | **Fixed**: drags, nudges and VoiceOver moves keep the centre on the canvas (`SnapEngine.clampedToCanvas`), in strip space for a panning collage. |
| P3 | On a panning collage, slots, captions and stickers the camera had left were still VoiceOver elements. | **Fixed**: `VideoCanvasView` lists only what the camera shows, and follows it. |
| P3 | Empty template: the template's caption covers the middle slot's "+" chip; on iPhone 17e with a panel open, "Add 3 clips" sits on the bottom slot's "+". | Open; "Add clips" still reaches every slot |
| P4 | iPhone 17e export sheet: the Image Quality control is cut off by the pinned action bar until you scroll. It can be reached, but nothing hints that the sheet scrolls. | Open |
